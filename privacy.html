Ara Privacy Policy

Last updated November 8, 2026

Who uses Ara

Ara is a personal assistant used only by its owner, Brandon Hahm, on his own computers. It has no other users and is not offered to the public.

What Google data Ara uses

  • Google Calendar: reads events, and adds or removes events when the owner asks.
  • Gmail: read-only. Ara can search and read messages but cannot send, delete or change email.
  • Email address: to tell the owner's connected accounts apart.

How the data is handled

  • It is used only to answer the owner's own questions and to prepare his daily briefing.
  • It is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • To answer a question, relevant text may be processed by the AI services Ara uses (such as Anthropic or OpenAI), under their API terms, which do not use it for training.

Ara's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
